What stays local

Templates, reference documents, employee details, contract history and outputs stay in this app’s own data folder. No AI engine, model download, account or online processing is required.

Keep the app’s folder outside iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox and other synced folders. Local storage is protected by your computer account; the database is not independently encrypted. Keep a private backup of the data and runtime folders.
